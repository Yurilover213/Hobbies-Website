Review: I really enjoy this Mahua's premise as this is the first time Ive seen a comic incoporate forensic science into a story, so much so that it plays quite an important role in the story. The tensions between both of the characters intrigued me a lot too. Their opposing personalities made for a very intersting story and I hope to see more on how their relationship would blossom in the upcoming chapters. This manhua provides a great mix of mystery and romance and I hope it continues keeping this standard of storytelling down the line.
